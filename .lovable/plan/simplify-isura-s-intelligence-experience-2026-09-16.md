# Simplify ISURA’s intelligence experience

## Goal
Keep the existing intelligence and trust engine intact, while making every user-facing conclusion understandable to a busy agency owner within seconds.

## What will change
- Replace visible AI, confidence, validation, evidence-strength, contradiction, and Guardian terminology with calm colleague-like language.
- Turn the client intelligence area into progressive disclosure: a short account summary first, concise reasons after “Why?”, and source excerpts only when explicitly opened.
- Show uncertainty naturally (“ISURA isn’t sure yet”) and explain the missing information without percentages or technical labels.
- Keep checks quiet by default and interrupt only for meaningful commitments, unsupported claims, changed client intent, financial promises, or significant risk.
- Simplify Drafts, Radar, and Approval Queue language so each item explains what happened, why it matters, and one clear next action.
- Remove configuration burden and technical explanations from everyday settings while preserving current behavior.
- Preserve all existing routes, mock data, persistence, decision rules, and legal pages.

## Technical details
- Keep the current signal model, deterministic validation, risk calculation, provenance, and audit data internal.
- Refactor only presentation components and user-facing copy; no backend or scoring changes.
- Retain accessible expanded states, keyboard controls, semantic headings, and reduced visual warning noise.
- Verify the main client detail, Radar, Drafts, Approvals, Settings, desktop, and mobile experiences after changes.
